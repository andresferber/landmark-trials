"Key toxicity" summarizes the main adverse events reported in the abstract (or the full paper where marked); it is not a complete safety profile. "★ Core" marks trials selected as essential for teaching. Each result is tagged "✓ abstract" or "✓ abstract + full paper" by what it was checked against. "Free full text" links open the article in PubMed Central; "Free at publisher" links open a publisher page that is free to read; "Publisher" links open the publisher's page, where a subscription or institutional access may be needed; "Free copy (repository)" links open a legal free copy in a university repository (sometimes the authors' manuscript). Free copies were found with Unpaywall (October 23, 2026). Registry links (NCT, ISRCTN or EudraCT numbers) open the trial's registry record and were taken from each paper's PubMed record. Source links go to the PubMed record (PMIDs checked against journal, volume, pages and first author). "Builds on", "Changed" and "Open questions" are interpretive summaries, not data from the papers. Commentaries are those PubMed links to each paper ("Comment in"). Regulatory dates were checked against FDA, EMA and sponsor announcements and published reports (October 23, 2026). Guideline positions summarize NCCN v5.2026 and the ASCO-Ontario 2026 living guideline at a high level; regimen-specific placements marked "NCCN not checked" could not be confirmed, because the NCCN guidelines require a login. No public data report insurance approval rates; US coverage generally follows the FDA label and NCCN compendium listing. Last checked: October 18, 2026.
