    Every result was checked against the paper's PubMed abstract on October 18, 2026; "not reported in abstract" means the figure needs the full text. Registry links (NCT, ISRCTN or EudraCT numbers) open the trial's registry record and were taken from each paper's PubMed record. Source links go to the PubMed record (PMIDs checked against journal, volume, pages and first author). "Builds on", "Changed" and "Open questions" are interpretive summaries, not data from the papers. Commentaries are those PubMed links to each paper ("Comment in"). Guideline positions: NCCN and ASCO-Ontario as summarized from January 2026 versions; items marked "verify" need checking against the current documents.
