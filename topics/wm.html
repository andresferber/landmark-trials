Landmark studies in Waldenström macroglobulinemia and lymphoplasmacytic lymphoma

Untreated and relapsed disease together: the consensus definition, prognostic scores and the MYD88 and CXCR4 genotypes; rituximab and chemoimmunotherapy; proteasome inhibitor regimens; and BTK inhibitors, venetoclax and pirtobrutinib: design, verified results, context, open questions and reception.

    Every result was checked against the paper's PubMed abstract on November 14, 2026; "not reported in abstract" means the figure needs the full text. "Key toxicity" summarizes the main adverse events reported in the abstract; it is not a complete safety profile. "Drug mechanism" links one recent review per drug or drug class on how it works and how resistance develops, chosen from peer-reviewed journals as the most recent, comprehensive and high-quality review available (no promotional material), with free full text preferred when quality was comparable. "★ Core" marks studies selected as essential for teaching. Studies included: untreated and relapsed disease are one topic, because most trials in this disease enrolled both. Only a few randomized trials exist, so the list holds every phase 3 or randomized trial with a full report, the phase 2 trials that were the basis of an approval or that defined a regimen in current use (whatever the journal), and the consensus definition, response criteria, prognostic models and genomic studies on which trials and practice rest. Ibrutinib-venetoclax, tirabrutinib, orelabrutinib, everolimus, Bing-Neel syndrome and histological transformation are not listed as cards. IgM MGUS and IgM-related disorders (neuropathy, cold agglutinin disease, cryoglobulinemia, amyloidosis) are not covered. Each result is tagged "✓ abstract" or "✓ abstract + full paper" by what it was checked against. "Free full text" links open the article in PubMed Central; "Free at publisher" links open a publisher page that is free to read; "Publisher" links open the publisher's page, where a subscription or institutional access may be needed; "Free copy (repository)" links open a legal free copy in a university repository (sometimes the authors' manuscript). Free copies were found with Unpaywall (November 14, 2026). Registry links (NCT numbers) open the trial's registry record and were taken from each paper's PubMed record or abstract. Source links go to the PubMed record (PMIDs checked against journal, volume, pages and first author). "Builds on", "Changed" and "Open questions" are interpretive summaries, not data from the papers. Commentaries are those PubMed links to each paper ("Comment in"); letters and author replies are not listed. FDA approval dates given in full were confirmed against FDA announcements or published reports, and the EU date for zanubrutinib against the EMA record (November 14, 2026); items marked "verify" could not be confirmed and will be checked at the next update. Guideline rows cite the International Workshop consensus reports; placements marked "NCCN not checked" could not be confirmed, because the NCCN guidelines require a login. No public data report insurance approval rates; US coverage generally follows the FDA label and NCCN compendium listing. Last checked: November 14, 2026.
